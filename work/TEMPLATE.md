# A sentence, not a label

Say what is wrong or missing, in the terms someone would use six months from
now. "CI does not gate the deploy" — not "CI".

## The problem

What actually breaks, and who feels it. Name the file, the setting, the query.
This is the part that stops being obvious first, so it is the part worth
writing while it is still fresh.

## The fix

The approach, not the diff. If a decision was already made, record it here
with the reason — a decision without its reason gets re-argued.

## Open

What is genuinely undecided, phrased as a question. An empty section here is a
fine outcome; a task with three of them is not ready to start.
